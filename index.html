THE MISSION

How to play

Explore colorful routes, collect stars, and answer three subject questions. In the boss arena, correct answers damage and stun the boss; incorrect answers show an explanation before a warning and dodgeable counterattack. You have three lives. Use X or SHOOT during a stun, and collect blue ammo crates.

⌨️

Move & jump

Use ← → or A / D to move. Press Space or ↑ to jump.

⭐

Collect stars

Gather stars along the path. Each one adds 5 points.

📚

Learn & win

Answer the three books, then face the level boss. Move with arrows or A/D, jump with Space, and hold X to fire.
